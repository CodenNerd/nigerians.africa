The problem

02

Two everyday failures of civic memory.

The typical Nigerian moves through a day of visible wrongs and invisible money. Neither leaves a durable public trail.

Spotlight 01 · Lawlessness

See it. Complain. Then nothing.

People experience and talk about blatant law violation — shakedowns, violence, official misconduct — but have no clear action item. The moment passes. The record does not form. Organisations that could prosecute never see the case in time.

Spotlight 02 · Local money

A CDA raises funds in the dark.

A community development association far from the capital collects dues and donations for local projects. The community stays vulnerable: there is no public record of who runs the CDA, how the money was raised, or how it was spent.

Why it stays broken

03

The pieces are published. The thread is missing.

A budget names a figure.

Allocations sit in documents that few people can search, and fewer can connect to a place or a community association they know.

A violation names no next step.

Complaints fill group chats. Evidence never becomes a matter. Legal NGOs never see the case while it is still fresh.

A report names a delay.

News and social posts move on. The evidence, the response, and the history do not stay attached.

People are asked to hold institutions accountable with fragments.

The solution

05

From a forgotten moment to a living case.

The product’s job is to catch what usually disappears — and leave a path open when someone is ready to pursue it.

Least case

Immortalise the little record.

A photo, a spend line, a named officer, a budget envelope — the everyday fragments journalists and organisations often miss until they matter. The platform keeps them attached to place, people, and money.

Best case

A matter reaches resolution.

Documented evidence becomes a Make Nigeria Better case. A vetted legal NGO picks it up and prosecutes until there is an outcome. The platform keeps the trail. Courts decide guilt.

Between those poles: follow a record by email, publish CDA people and spend, walk budget → allocation → project, and ask the graph what happened.

Trust and accuracy

09

Every serious claim carries a trail.

Status is on the page.

Official record, reported, under review, disputed, AI Generated. A reader can see what kind of statement they are looking at.

Sources are required.

Money, claims, office-holder history, and evidence are refused when a source link is missing. The check runs before the record updates.

A person reviews first.

Public data lands in a staging area. A reviewer approves it before it enters the record. The crawler never writes the live record directly.

AI explains. It cites.

Ask retrieves from the graph, then summarises. Thin evidence is described as thin. The model does not invent a source or a verdict.

Sources in this proof of concept: Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons, the Budget Office of the Federation, curated government pages, and citizen submissions. Each carries a publisher and a link.

Real conditions

10

Useful where the connection is thin and the stakes are high.

Trust

Who said it, when, what supports it, and whether the status later changed. History stays visible.

Low bandwidth

Maps have list fallbacks. The record is text, status, and links, readable when images are too heavy.

Inclusion

Plain-language guidance, semantic pages, a skip link, and status a screen reader can name.

Privacy

Reading is open. Submitting requires sign-in. Guidance says to capture only what is lawful and safe.

Languages

English in this prototype. Translations can sit beside the graph — Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Pidgin — without rewriting relationships.

Local reality

States, local governments, wards, offices, and laws are specific. Another community reuses the model with its own institutions.

AI coding

12

Built with AI coding tools. Grounded in the public record.

Inside the product

  • Ask retrieves matching records from the graph before any summary is written.
  • Answers are labelled AI Generated and cite the pages they used.
  • With no model key, the same question still returns the structured record.
  • The model is instructed to point back to citations and to avoid verdicts.

How the prototype was made

  • The civic idea — a public memory people can follow and act on — was set by the team.
  • AI coding tools in Cursor implemented the domain graph, the public interface, ingestion, and the connected seed.
  • Judges can run the journey locally: install, start, and open the Allen Avenue thread.
  • The proof of concept is the argument: every documented path is navigable.
